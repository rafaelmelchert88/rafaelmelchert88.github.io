Values

These are the values I try to practise in life and work. They are inspired by Stoic philosophy, which centres on wisdom, courage, justice, and temperance. I think of temperance in more practical terms: discipline and self-control.

The definitions below are adapted from Oxford Languages. The video below is a useful introduction to the four Stoic virtues.

  • Discipline

    • The controlled behaviour resulting from such training.
    • Activity that provides mental or physical training.
    • A system of rules of conduct

  • Self-Control

    • The ability to control oneself, in particular one's emotions and desires, especially in difficult situations

  • Justice

    • Just behaviour or treatment
    • The quality of being fair and reasonable

  • Courage

    • The ability to do something that frightens one, bravery.
    • Strength in the face of pain or grief.

  • Wisdom

    • The quality of having experience, knowledge, and good judgement, the quality of being wise.
    • The fact of being based on sensible or wise thinking
    • The body of knowledge and experience that develops within a specified society or period.
