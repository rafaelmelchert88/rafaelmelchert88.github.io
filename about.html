About Rafael Melchert

Hello there! My name is Rafael Melchert from Florianópolis, in Santa Catarina, Brazil.

Since I was little, I've been fascinated by figuring out how things work—whether in technology, books, or self-improvement. I delve into the reasons behind everything, always eager to learn and evolve.

When it comes to solving problems, I prefer to keep things simple, working toward solutions that are easy to execute, adapt to different situations, and maintain high performance.

When I'm not working, I enjoy spending time outdoors or getting lost in a good book. Stoic philosophy resonates with me. I aim to apply its principles of discipline, resilience, and mindfulness to my life.

Lately, I've been building this website, where I share my stories and connect with others to explore ideas together. I'm always excited to collaborate on new projects and have meaningful conversations that inspire growth.

Feel free to reach out. I'm always open to new opportunities and interesting discussions.
